# Code of Conduct

Drum Spike adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, for every issue, pull request, discussion and other project space.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, welcome people of every
background and level of experience, and keep criticism about the work, not the person.
Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through the contact form at
[cherry-blossom-valley.com](https://cherry-blossom-valley.com/#contact). Reports are handled in
confidence. The maintainers decide what follows, as described in the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
