9. Doubtful hits first

After the analysis, some hits are less sure than others: a quiet hit, a hit a rule added. The review queue puts these in front of you, so you check the doubtful hits before you export.

The app gives every hit a confidence from 0 to 1. It comes from the level of the hit against the median of its track: a hit as loud as the median gets 1, a hit 24 dB under the median gets 0. A hit that you added or moved keeps confidence 1, and a hit that is grey does not count. A hit under 0.5 is doubtful.

Steps

  1. Press D for Hits > Next Doubtful Hit. The queue holds the doubtful hits of all tracks, lowest confidence first. The playhead jumps to the first one and the app selects it. The status line says which doubtful hit you are on, for example doubtful hit 3 of 17.
  2. Press Shift+D for Hits > Previous Doubtful Hit to step back. With no doubtful hit left, the status line says no more doubtful hits.
  3. Read the info line under the toolbar, above the lanes: the Doubt field says the confidence of the hit and the reasons, for example 0.25: 18 dB under the track median. A hit that the level gate alone would have refused names the rescue that kept it, for example soft gate: ghost note below the level gate, shape rescue below the level gate or weak flux, kept by the time-domain attack. This tells you what to check.
  4. Check the hit: press A for Hits > Audition Hit, and look at the attack in the lane (chapter 4). A doubtful hit that is a real hit stays as it is; one that is not, you delete with Delete, or nudge with Left and Right (chapter 7). Edit the hit and its confidence no longer matters: your edit decides.
  5. When the queue is empty, the status line says no doubtful hits. Export as in chapter 5.

The review queue only points at hits. It never changes the detection, and a hit you do not touch keeps its place in the exports.
