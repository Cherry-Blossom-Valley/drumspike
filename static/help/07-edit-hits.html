7. Edit hits

The detector is right almost always. When it is not, you fix the hit list by hand: add a hit, delete a hit, or move a hit by samples. Every edit is undoable, and the exports follow.

Steps

  1. Put the playhead where the hit is missing and press H for Edit > Add Hit at Playhead. The new hit sits on the playhead, on the selected track.
  2. Select a hit and press Delete or Backspace for Edit > Delete Hit. Or right-click the hit.
  3. Select a hit and press Left or Right for Edit > Nudge Hit Earlier and Nudge Hit Later. The step is the nudge value: the list Edit > Nudge Value > 1 Sample holds 1 Sample, 10 Samples, 100 Samples, 1 ms and 10 ms. One sample is the smallest step; the exports carry it. Shift+Left and Shift+Right nudge by ten times the nudge value.
  4. Press A for Hits > Audition Hit: the selected hit plays on its track alone, from 30 ms before to 250 ms after the hit. This is how you check a nudge. Shift+A loops the hit until Escape or Space; with a range selected, A loops the range. When Audition on Tab is on (the ear button in the toolbar, or Transport > Audition on Tab, key U), Tab and the other keys that step to a hit play it too.
  5. A hit that the rules keep but you do not want (or the other way round): select it and press X for Hits > Toggle Hit Active. The hit is deactivated or back. Your choice wins over the automatic rules and stays after a new analysis, a threshold change and a Learn change; the exports, MIDI, spikes and clicks follow it. A deactivated hit shows grey with a dashed line.
  6. Undo with Edit > Undo (Cmd+Z), redo with Shift+Cmd+Z. Edit > Undo History… (Option+Cmd+Z) lists the steps; a click goes back to that step.

Edits stay edits: Drum Spike never changes your audio. Export again after an edit and the DAW gets the new files (chapter 5).
