4. Sample-exact

This is the core of the product. Detection says "a hit is near here". Drum Spike then walks to the last quiet sample before the first motion of the stroke and puts the hit on that sample. The first motion counts in either direction: a stroke and its polarity-flipped copy land on the same sample. Your triggered sound starts on the same sample as the drum on the close mic.

A new analysis runs the current placement rule version; a project keeps the rule version of its analysis until a new analysis. Today that is placement rule version 10.

Steps

  1. Zoom to View > Zoom Presets > 5 ms (key 6) to see it.
  2. Click a hit. The hit line sits on the first motion of the attack, not on the loudest point.
  3. Move between hits with the Next Hit and Previous Hit keys to spot-check a few. Chapter 9 takes you to the doubtful ones first.

Nothing to adjust here in the normal case. The sample-exact position is the default, not an option you have to find. If a hit should sit elsewhere, the Inspector's Hitpoint shift moves it: the shift is a number of samples (positive = later, negative = earlier) that moves the current hits of the track at once and is stored for the next analysis.
