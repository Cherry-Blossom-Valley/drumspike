6. Find your way: play, zoom, move between hits

You have hits (chapters 2 to 4) and files (chapter 5). Now you check them by ear and by eye. Drum Spike moves like a DAW: play, zoom, and jump from hit to hit. The demo session holds 309 hits over 62.5 s; these keys take you through them fast.

Steps

  1. Press Space. Transport > Play/Stop plays from the playhead; press Space again to stop. Option+Space is play-and-return: the next press of Option+Space or Space stops and puts the playhead back where the play started. The keys reach the app from anywhere: after a click on a button or a slider (the Sensitivity slider of a histogram, for example) Space still plays. Only a text field keeps its keys, and the status line tells you when a transport key went into one. While the computer is busy (a large build, for example), the playhead keeps moving smoothly: frames drawn between two clock updates no longer lose the playhead. Transport > Stop Returns to Start (I) is a mode like Suspend Follow when Editing: while it is checked, a Stop (Space while playing, or the ■ button) puts the playhead back where the play started, exactly like Option+Space; the setting survives a restart.
  2. Press Shift+Space for Transport > Half-Speed Play: half speed, one octave down. This is the setting for a close check of an attack.
  3. Zoom with the number keys. The six presets of View > Zoom Presets > Full (1), 30 s (2), 5 s (3), 500 ms (4), 50 ms (5) and 5 ms (6) set the span of the lanes. View > Zoom In (T) and View > Zoom Out (R) zoom around the playhead.
  4. Change the track height with Option+Up and Option+Down (View > Track Height > Shorter, Taller), or set it directly with Option+Cmd+1, Option+Cmd+2 and Option+Cmd+3 (Small, Medium, Large). Drag the lower border of a track row or of its header to give one track its own height; a double-click on the border puts it back.
  5. The lane toggles: View > Velocity Lane (V), View > Other Mics Under Hit (O), View > Bars|Beats Ruler (Shift+B) and View > Markers Ruler (Option+Shift+R; #265 moved Shift+R to Hits > Re-analyze Selection).
  6. Step through the hits. Hits > Next Hit (Tab) and Hits > Previous Hit (L) move on the selected track; with no track selected, they move on all tracks. Hits > Next Hit on All Tracks (N) always moves on all tracks.
  7. Press Option+O for View > Show Overview: the whole session in one strip over the lanes. Click it to move fast over a long session.
  8. Press M for Edit > Set Marker at Selection to drop a marker at the selected hit or at the playhead. Transport > Next Marker (Option+M) comes back to it later.

The keys of this chapter are the Pro Tools preset, the default. Drum Spike also has a Cubase preset: Drum Spike > Settings… (Cmd+,) switches. Help > Keyboard Shortcuts lists all keys of the current preset. Drum Spike > About Drum Spike shows the Cherry Blossom Valley logo, the licence line and links to the website and the source code.
