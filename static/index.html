Settings

Audio output
Keys
Pro Tools is the default set. Cubase changes the keys of the hits, the zoom, the nudge, mute, solo and the transport (Help > Keyboard Shortcuts lists them).
View
E zooms to the selected range (or hit). When this is on, E also doubles the height of the track that holds it; E again puts the zoom, the scroll position and the heights back.
Audition
A plays the selected hit on its track alone (30 ms before to 250 ms after); Shift+A loops until Escape or Space; a range selection loops the range. When this is on, Tab and the other keys that step to a hit also play it.
Metronome click
Plays a short beep on every hit of audible tracks during playback. The pitch is set per role below, so you can hear which lane plays.
Click pitches (Hz)
