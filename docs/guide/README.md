# Drum Spike guide, part 1: the main job

Part 1 of the guide walks the main path: bring tracks in, find the hits, put each hit on the
first sample of its attack, and export MIDI and spike files. Part 2 (chapters 6 to 10) is about
checking and fixing. Parts 3 and 4 cover what the app does on its own and for power users.

The demo session is the example in every chapter: 13 tracks, 62.5 s, 309 hits.
