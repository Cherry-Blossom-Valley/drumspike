Changes since 0.9.5:

### Lanes
- Each track header shows a drawing of its instrument role (kick, snare, tom, hat, cymbal, room or clap mic). A role change swaps the drawing at once.
- The Analyze button shows a waveform icon and a running state while the analysis works.

### Detection
- A detected hit now sits on the last quiet sample before the wave starts to move. On real recordings most kick and tom hits move 1 or 2 samples earlier than in 0.9.5, and snare hits 1 to 5 samples. No hit moves later. Projects analysed before keep their hits until Re-analyze.

### Guide
- The guide chapters on export, finding your way and doubtful hits match the app again.
